# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in JalRakshak welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying precise about risks and limitations.
- Prefer evidence-backed claims over hype.
- Protect user privacy and never post secrets or sensitive personal data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private credentials, tokens, keys, account details, or personal data.
- Misrepresenting the prototype as an official emergency, medical, or public-safety system.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.
