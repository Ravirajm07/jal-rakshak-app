# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1, adapted for this repository.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in JalRakshak welcoming and harassment-free for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and respect.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information, credentials, tokens, or citizen data.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Misrepresenting this prototype as an official emergency, medical, or public-safety system.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. They may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Reporting

A dedicated code-of-conduct reporting channel must be configured by the maintainers before broad community launch. Until then, use GitHub's repository moderation/reporting tools or contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Do not use public issues to report private conduct concerns.
